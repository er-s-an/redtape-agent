Three hundred million people live across borders. Their lives run on a chain of documents: passport, visa, status, license. Renewing one requires the others to be valid — and every renewal has a window, a lead time, and a wait. Miss one, and it cascades: you can't renew your license without your passport, and your passport is sitting at a consulate for eight weeks. Reminder apps tell you a date is coming. Nothing guards the chain. RedTape does.
